Step 4 of 5
What is your soil like?
This helps ZField give you accurate fertilizer timing and moisture advice.
🏖
Sandy soil
Light, drains fast, dries out quickly. Common in lower elevation areas. Needs more frequent watering and fertilizer.
🌱
Loam soil
Dark, crumbly, holds moisture well. The best soil for most crops. Good drainage and nutrient retention.
🟤
Clay soil
Heavy, sticky when wet, cracks when dry. Retains water for a long time. Common in low-lying areas and river valleys.
⚫
Black cotton soil
Very expansive, very sticky when wet. Waterlogging risk. Common in parts of the Lower Shire and lake shore areas.
🤷
Not sure
That is fine. ZField will use general recommendations and ask you a few simple questions to learn your soil better.
