What ZField does

Not a chatbot.
A precision farming advisor.

🌡

Weather-driven recommendations

ZField pulls live weather data for your exact location — rainfall, temperature, humidity, forecast — and combines it with your crop stage and soil type to tell you the right thing to do at the right time. Not when the calendar says. When your field is ready.

Example
"Your cassava is showing early moisture stress. Rainfall forecast drops off for 12 days. Mulch around the base within 48 hours to retain what moisture is left."
📷

Crop disease diagnosis

Upload a photo of your crop. ZField identifies the disease, pest, or deficiency and gives you specific, locally available treatment steps.

🌱

Personalized farm profile

Tell ZField your crop, planting date, soil type, and location once. Every recommendation after that is built around your specific situation.

🗣

Your language

Ask questions and receive advice in Chichewa, Chitumbuka, Yao, Lomwe, or English. ZField responds in the language you choose.

📊

Market prices

Weekly farmgate prices for every major crop at ADMARC and local markets across Malawi. Know before you sell.

🚨

Pest and weather alerts

Regional pest outbreak warnings, dry spell advisories, and flood risk alerts — pulled from government and NGO sources automatically.

How it works

Three steps to smarter farming.

01

Set up your farm profile

Tell ZField what you are growing, when you planted it, what your soil is like, and where your farm is. This takes about two minutes and you only do it once.

02

ZField reads your conditions

ZField automatically pulls live weather data for your district, tracks your crop growth stage, and monitors regional pest and disease alerts.

03

Get advice built for your field

Ask anything or let ZField prompt you when something needs your attention. Every answer is specific to your crop, your soil, and your weather — not generic advice from a textbook.
